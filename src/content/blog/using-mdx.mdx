You do not need a destination to have a good nature walk. Pick a nearby route, leave a little time to wander, and let your attention—not your step count—set the pace.

## Try the three-stop pause

At the start, stop and listen for one minute. A little farther on, notice a detail you might normally pass by: a leaf pattern, a patch of moss, or the direction the clouds are moving. Before you head home, look back and see what has changed.

Keep to paths where possible and observe wildlife from a distance. Taking a quick note or sketch can help you remember the moment without collecting anything from the place.
